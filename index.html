why Babe Club

You deserve more than the same Friday night, on repeat.

Babe Club started because making friends as an adult is harder than anyone admits, and this town has more going on than you'd think. We plan the events, find the local partnerships, and make sure you're always in the loop. All you have to do is show up.

social

Social events

Girls' nights, themed parties, and get-togethers built to turn "we should hang out" into an actual plan on the calendar.

wellness

Wellness meetups

Pilates, wellness mornings, and low-key ways to take care of yourself alongside women who get it.

networking

Business networking

Real connections with women building their own thing — no stiff mixers, just genuine, useful relationships.

At the end of the day, it's girlhood, with a cherry on top.
