Our story

This Is Babe Club

A community of women showing up for each other — no cliques, no algorithm, just real connection.

Babe Club started with a simple idea: the best nights happen when you close the app and actually show up. What began as a small group of friends hosting sunset hangs turned into a Southern California community built on the same energy — welcoming, low-pressure, and always a little bit magic.

Real Over Curated

We're not chasing a highlight reel. Every event is a chance to meet the girls behind the likes.

Come As You Are

No cliques, no dress code for who belongs. Just welcoming spaces to show up exactly as yourself.

Rooted In SoCal

From sunset terraces to cozy nights in, every gathering is a love letter to our home coast.

Why Offline?

We spend enough of our lives scrolling. Babe Club exists to pull us back into the room — the actual room, with actual people, where friendships get to happen in real time.

So we host. Dinners, beach days, golden hour socials, weekend getaways — anything that gets a group of women in one place, phones down, laughing about something that'll never make it to a caption.
